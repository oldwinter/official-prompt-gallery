documentation and, after private admission, AI-generated video outputs. The
prompts remain attributed to their publishers. Source links are provided for
verification; this repository does not imply endorsement by either publisher.

capability-aligned record, not a benchmark, leaderboard, recommendation, or

to each output. A missing served-model, seed, or cost field is represented by a
named evidence variant rather than an invented value.
